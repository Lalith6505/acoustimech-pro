Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026
Application Name: AcoustiMech Pro
Package Name: com.acoustimech.pro

1. Overview & Commitment to Zero-Cloud Privacy

AcoustiMech Pro is an industrial engineering and vibration diagnostic utility developed for professional use in confidential maritime, industrial, power-generation, and defense facilities. We operate with an uncompromising zero-cloud privacy policy: we do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data, telemetry, device identifiers, or usage logs.

2. Sensor & Hardware Permissions

To perform its mechanical diagnostic functions, the application requests the following Android permissions:

  • HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS & Accelerometer (android.hardware.sensor.accelerometer): Required to capture high-frequency physical mechanical vibrations (up to 500+ Hz) when the smartphone is held against rotating machinery casings. All accelerometer data is processed strictly in volatile device RAM for real-time FFT calculation and is never transmitted off the device.
  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone): Used exclusively when the user explicitly enables "Acoustic Ultrasound Mode" to capture high-frequency acoustic friction, gear impacts, and pump cavitation (44.1 kHz PCM). Raw audio streams are processed solely in real-time memory for FFT spectral analysis. Audio is NEVER recorded to audio files, saved to storage, or transmitted across any network.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for brief tactile haptic feedback when vibration levels exceed ISO 10816 Zone D critical trip thresholds.

3. Local Data Storage

Any inspection logs saved by the user (such as Machine Name, Measurement Point, and RMS velocity values) are stored entirely on the local device using SQLite. Users retain 100% control over this data and may export it via CSV or delete it at any time.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

AcoustiMech Pro is a one-time paid application. It contains:

  • ZERO third-party advertising SDKs (no AdMob, Unity, etc.).
  • ZERO analytics or telemetry trackers (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, etc.).
  • ZERO account requirements or logins.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application is an industrial diagnostic tool and does not address or knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Since the application does not transmit data, our zero-data collection guarantee remains permanent.
